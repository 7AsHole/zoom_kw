How this works

  1. Turn on your camera & mic — tap the camera icon in the bottom bar and allow access.
  2. Start or join a call — tap the phone icon to start a new call, or paste a Call ID and tap the checkmark to join one.
  3. Share the call — tap the link icon to copy an invite link for others.
  4. Tap a tile to focus it — tap any participant's video to make it big; tap it again to go back to the grid.
  5. Zoom your own view — the magnifying glass on your own tile zooms your camera or screen share in and out. Only you can control it.
  6. Bottom bar — mute, camera on/off, flip camera (phones with two cameras), share screen (desktop only), and hang up.
